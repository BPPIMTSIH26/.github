### 1. 🌊 SIH26057 — S.A.G.A.R. (Sonar Anomaly Geospatial Analytical Reconnaissance)
* **Repository:** [`SIH26057`](https://github.com/BPPIMTSIH26/SIH26057)
* **Lead:** **Narayan Kumar Jha** ([@narayan-nkj](https://github.com/narayan-nkj))
* **Mission:** Autonomous underwater anomaly intelligence for Side-Scan Sonar (SSS) and Synthetic Aperture Sonar (SAS). Integrates a 14-stage acoustic despeckling and CLAHE preprocessing pipeline, open-world Mahalanobis novelty scoring, Ultralytics YOLOv8 real-time hazard identification, sub-meter geospatial dead-reckoning calibration, and 3D bathymetric visualization.

### 2. ❄️ SIH26060 — NCPOR Polar Digital Twin
* **Repository:** [`SIH26060`](https://github.com/BPPIMTSIH26/SIH26060)
* **Live Deployment:** [ncporpolartwin.vercel.app](https://ncporpolartwin.vercel.app)
* **Mission:** Real-time environmental sensing, renewable power microgrid management, logistical auto-resupply scheduling, and structural health telemetry simulation for India's scientific research stations in Antarctica and the Arctic.

## 🛠️ Core Engineering Standards

All repositories under `@BPPIMTSIH26` adhere to strict production standards:
* **Microservices & Clean Architecture:** Strictly decoupled backend and frontend environments with dedicated data contracts.
* **Security & Defense Hardening:** Strict role-based access control, cryptographic verification, rate limiting, and zero hardcoded secrets.
* **Type Safety & Determinism:** Full TypeScript strict typing across frontend interfaces and Pydantic validation schemas across REST APIs.
* **Comprehensive Documentation:** Full architectural diagrams, API contracts, and reproduction guides.
